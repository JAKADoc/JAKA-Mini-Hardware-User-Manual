3. Manual Instruction

3.1. About the Manual

The manual contains:

  • Precautions for the use of JAKA Mini robots.

  • Installation and commissioning of JAKA Mini robots.

  • Cleaning and maintenance of JAKA Mini robots.

3.2. Manual Reader

The manual is intended for:

  • Operators.

  • Commissioning staff.

  • Maintenance staff.

  • Integrators.

3.3. References

Documentation referred to in the manual:

  • JAKA Coboπ Software User Manual.

  • JAKA Service Manual.

Note

The documents above are available from the JAKA website at www.jaka.com.

3.4. Prerequisites

The reader should:

  • Be trained and certified by JAKA and have the required knowledge of mechanical and electrical installation/repair/maintenance work.

  • Be trained to respond to emergencies or abnormal situations.
