Installation Environment
==========================

Install the control cabinet in a dry, well-ventilated location. The cabinet is cooled by natural convection. The figure below shows the minimum clearance required for installation.

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/安装环境要求.*
    :align: center
    :width: 19cm

    Installation environment requirements

.. warning::

   - Ensure that there is sufficient clearance around the control cabinet for heat dissipation, particularly when it is installed inside an enclosure.
   - Do not install the cabinet in damp environments or where it may be exposed to splashing water.
   - Do not use the cabinet in an enclosed space. Poor ventilation can cause the cabinet to overheat, reduce its service life, or even damage it.
   - Keep the cabinet away from heat sources such as furnaces.
   - Do not use this product near corrosive or flammable gases, combustible materials, or in environments containing hydrogen sulfide, chlorine, ammonia, sulfur, chlorinated gases, acids, alkalis, or salts.
   - Use the MiniCab at altitudes below 1,000 m (3281 ft). For use above 1,000 m (3281 ft), derating is required.

.. _Installation Procedure:

Installation Procedure
=======================

.. list-table:: Required installation tools
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 1

   * - **Tool**
     - **Specification**

   * - Hex socket screw
     - M3

   * - Allen key
     - 2.5 mm

   * - Electric drill
     - 3 mm drill bit

Follow the instructions below to install the cabinet.

Both the bottom panel and side panel of the control cabinet have four M3 threaded mounting holes, 
allowing for different mounting methods. The bottom panel is 4 mm thick and the side panel is 6 mm thick. 
The following example shows surface mounting.
For DIN-rail mounting, provide rail-mounting hardware compatible with the control cabinet.

**Mounting on the bottom panel:**

  - Drill four 3 mm holes in the mounting surface according to the control cabinet outline drawing.

      .. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/底面安装-打孔.*
          :align: center
          :width: 12cm

  - Align the four holes in the bottom enclosure with the holes in the mounting surface, then secure the control cabinet with screws.

      .. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/底面安装-固定螺钉.*
          :align: center
          :width: 12cm

  - Ensure that the mounting surface is level and free of vibration, and verify that the control cabinet is securely mounted. To reduce the effects of vibration during movement, rubber pads are recommended between the control cabinet and the mounting surface.

**Mounting on the side panel:**

  - Drill four 3 mm holes in the mounting surface according to the control cabinet outline drawing.
  
      .. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/侧面安装-打孔.*
          :align: center
          :width: 12cm

  - Align the four holes in the side enclosure with the holes in the mounting surface, then secure the control cabinet with screws.

      .. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/侧面安装-固定螺钉.*
          :align: center
          :width: 12cm

  - Ensure that the mounting surface is level and free of vibration, and verify that the control cabinet is securely mounted. To reduce the effects of vibration during movement, rubber pads are recommended between the control cabinet and the mounting surface.
